CredCloud

Privacy

For CredCloud (credcloud.app) and CredCloud Helper. Effective September 29, 2026.

In short

  • CredCloud stores the credentialing records your workspace enters, so your team can keep them up to date and fill applications with them.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.
  • SSNs, CAQH passwords, two-step secrets and uploaded documents are encrypted before they're stored.
  • CredCloud Helper fills payer portal forms in Chrome on your own computer. It never sees your portal passwords and never submits a form.

What CredCloud stores

  • Your account. Name, email, role and workspace. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash. If you use two-step sign-in, its secret is encrypted and its recovery codes are hashed. We record failed sign-ins to lock out guessing.
  • What your workspace enters. Provider and practice records: names, contact details, NPIs, dates of birth, licenses, certifications, education, work and training history, malpractice coverage and claims, disclosures, hospital privileges, references and payer enrollments. SSNs and CAQH passwords are encrypted and shown only through a logged action.
  • Documents you upload. Encrypted by CredCloud, then stored in a storage bucket used only by your workspace. A deleted document can be recovered for 30 days, then it's gone.
  • Applications and portal fills. PDF templates and the applications made from them. Portal templates, meaning which box on a portal gets which answer. The answers for a portal fill are encrypted while the fill is open and cleared when it ends; we keep only which boxes were filled.
  • Activity records. Who viewed an SSN, a CAQH password, a document or an application, and when. A history of changes to records, so your team can see who changed what.
  • Technical data. A session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a security token for forms. IP addresses in the activity records above and in security logs. Cloudflare, which protects the site, may set its own security cookies.

CredCloud is for credentialing records about providers and practices. Don't enter patient health information.

CredCloud Helper

  • On your computer it keeps a token that connects it to your CredCloud account, and a Chrome profile of its own for payer portals, so your portal sign-ins stay on your computer. Your everyday Chrome profile isn't touched.
  • On a portal page it opened for you, it adds a CredCloud panel. When you press Fill this page, it types that provider's answers into the boxes on the page. It doesn't read what's on the page, and it doesn't read or store your portal password.
  • When you teach it a portal, it reads the label of each box you click and the page's address path, and sends them to CredCloud as the portal template.
  • It sends CredCloud only the portal templates you save, which boxes a fill filled, and which kind of computer it runs on, so it can keep itself up to date. It doesn't collect your browsing history.
  • To stop it, disconnect the computer under My account > CredCloud Helper, or uninstall it.

How we use it

Only to run CredCloud for your workspace: to store and show your records, fill applications and portal forms, keep your account secure, and send account email such as join approvals, password resets and security notices. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to decide anyone's credit or eligibility for anything.

Who else handles it

These services run CredCloud for us, and handle data only to do that:

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the app, its database, document storage and backups, in the United States.
  • Cloudflare carries traffic to the site and protects it from attacks.
  • Google delivers CredCloud's email, through Gmail.

We don't share data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How it's protected

  • Every connection uses HTTPS.
  • Each workspace's data is kept apart from every other workspace's, and each workspace's documents are in their own bucket.
  • Admins must use two-step sign-in, and repeated wrong passwords lock an account for a while.
  • Backups are encrypted before they leave the server, with a key that isn't kept on it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your workspace's data while the workspace is in use. Your workspace's admins can delete records, documents and accounts at any time, and a whole workspace can be deleted on request.

Nightly encrypted backups are kept for 30 days, and one backup from each month for about 13 months. Deleted data can remain in those backups until they expire. They're used only to recover from a failure.

Your choices

You can see and correct most of what CredCloud holds about you in the app, or ask your workspace's admin. To ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted, contact us below. If your workspace's admin added you, we may need their agreement, since the records belong to the workspace.
