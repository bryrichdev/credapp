CredCloud Helper

A small program on your computer that fills payer portals in Chrome with provider data from CredCloud. You set it up once; after that, Fill and Teach in CredCloud open the portal for you, and it keeps itself up to date. It never submits a form, and your portal sign-ins stay on your computer.
